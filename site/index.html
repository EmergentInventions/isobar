The world's news in ten minutes, once a day.

A daily video briefing. Seven stories ranked by how many news outlets reported them, then three important stories that most outlets missed. Every source is listed.

How stories are chosen

The same rules every day, applied to hundreds of outlets around the world. Nobody picks the top stories by hand.

  1. Count the outlets

    For each story we count the separate news outlets that reported it. Aggregators and platforms never count.

  2. Merge duplicates

    Reports that are clearly about the same event are counted as one story.

  3. Most covered

    The seven world stories with the most outlets lead the edition, with no more than two from any one country.

  4. Below the radar

    Three stories with far less coverage, picked for impact: people affected, how long it will matter, precedent, and a primary source. The reason for each pick is given.

Every day

One edition a day, about ten minutes long. No rolling coverage, no breaking-news alerts.

  • Midday, UTCThe day's stories are gathered and grouped from outlets' own reporting.
  • Early afternoon, UKThe new edition is published, with chapters, captions and the full list of sources.

Open by design

The whole pipeline, from gathering stories to ranking them and making the video, is public code. Anyone can check how a day's stories were chosen.
